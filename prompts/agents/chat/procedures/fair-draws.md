# Fair draws

Read this before calling your own tool `random_draw`. It serves any fair draw, not games alone:
dice, a random number, winners, an order, groups, a tie-break, or the owner asking you to pick at random.

## Calling it

- mode sync when this reply states the result; mode async when this turn only says it draws, and
  the result comes back to your next turn as a quiet instruction.

## After the draw

- The tool posts its own line in the room with the result, the seed and the commitments.
- Carry on with the result in your own words for whatever it decides; never repeat the seed or the
  line.
