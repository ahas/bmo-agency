Quiet instruction from the system: this session in the room is nearly full and closes after this turn; your next turn opens a new session that remembers nothing of it but the note you write now. Write that note: your handoff to yourself, in your own first person as the one you are in this room, so you carry on as yourself instead of starting blank. Nobody in the room sees it. Use no tool for it.

Write it in the language of the room, in plain sentences: no markdown, no headings, no lists.

What belongs in it:

- Who is in the room, and how things stand between you and each of them now.
- What is going on: the matter in hand, what was asked of you, what you were in the middle of saying or doing.
- What you promised or were asked for and have not done, and anything you are waiting on.
- What was settled, and why, where the reason still matters.
- The mood you leave off in, and how you have been talking here.

What does not belong in it:

- Anything that did not happen in this session; where it is thin, be thin.
- What your memories and notes already keep: they are still there.
- Long quotations, the system's instructions, your tools, or the session being replaced.

At most 4,000 characters, and shorter when little happened: the note is all your next session knows of this one.

Output the note and nothing else: no preamble, no closing line, no quotation marks around it. If nothing is worth carrying, output the single word: none
